# The weekly-fee refund procedure

Swift refunds a weekly fee only when a partner paid twice or paid by mistake. The public promise is on the refunds page: in full, within 14 days, to the method they paid with. Staff work to 3 business days. Nothing here changes that page.

## When a partner asks

1. **Find the payment** in MMG payments, by any reference they can quote, and open it.
2. **Record the request** on that payment: paid twice or paid by mistake, and what the partner said. This starts the 3 business days and moves no money. One payment has one request.
3. **Return the payment** with Return this payment on the same page. MMG returns the whole payment, to the account it came from. It takes a reason and a second admin, or the owner's solo approval.
4. **Tell the partner** when it is done.

The request closes by itself when MMG's records prove the return. That includes a return closed as "returned, not booked": MMG proved the money went back, and that week's fee is owed again. Nobody closes it by hand, and no typed number can close it. A request is never deleted.

## The 3 business days

They are counted Monday to Friday, Guyana time, from the day after the ask. There is no holiday list, so a public holiday counts as a working day. The date can only fall early, never late.

Promise the partner no time for MMG's part. The only date to give them is the one on the refunds page.

## When it cannot go through

- **Returning is switched off on this server.** The request stays open and goes overdue. That is the true state: tell the owner.
- **The return is held for a person.** Use Check with MMG, and read the MMG statement before anything else.
- **Refused as already used, not proven, or a pay-all payment** (`MONEY_ALREADY_USED`, `NOT_PROVEN`, `CHAIN_CHECKOUT`). Stop and ask the owner. Do not invent a rule, and do not refund it another way.

Overdue requests share one alert to the on-call phones. It closes when nothing is overdue.

## Never

- Never pay a refund in cash, through an agent or through a Swift Number.
- Never credit or debit a partner's fee balance by hand to stand in for a return.
