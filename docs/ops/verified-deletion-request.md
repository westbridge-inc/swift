# A deletion request from someone who cannot sign in

The public deletion page tells people who cannot sign in to email privacy@swiftgy.com, and says a verified request is completed within one month. This is how staff do it.

1. **Note the date the request arrived.** The month runs from then.
2. **Find the account** in People. If there is none, say so in your reply and stop.
3. **Check it is their account.**
   - The normal way: on the person's page use Show number (it asks for a reason and is recorded), text a one-time code to that number from the Swift support number, and ask them to send it back on the same email thread.
   - Anything else goes to the owner: a lost number, no access to it, or any doubt. The owner decides how to check it, or refuses.
   - Never ask for a copy of an identity document. If one is sent anyway, delete it and do not record it.
4. **Ask for the deletion.** Only a super admin can. On the person's page choose Delete account, pick how identity was checked, say what was done in words, and give the reason. Leave out ID numbers, phone numbers and email addresses: the record is permanent.
5. **Approval.** It waits in Approvals for a second admin. While the owner is the only admin, the owner's solo approval applies: the wait, a fresh sign-in code and the notices.
6. **Run it.** Once it is approved, the admin who asked runs it from Approvals.
7. **If it is refused, nothing was deleted.** Live orders, live jobs or open cash must finish first. Tell the person what is left, and ask again once it has cleared.
8. **Reply to the person** when it is done. Financial and legal records are kept, as the privacy policy says.

A staff account is never closed this way.
